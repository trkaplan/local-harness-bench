All three bugs fixed in `ledger/core.py`, with a regression test for each in `tests/test_core.py`. **8/8 tests pass** (`python -m pytest -q`).

## Fixes

**1. `parse_amount` — accounting negative + minus sign** (`ledger/core.py:17`)
The `negative = not negative` toggle made `-(45.00)` flip back to *positive*, and since the `-` was stripped before `Decimal(s)` ran, the actual failure was `InvalidOperation` leaking out of `Decimal` — not a `ValueError`. Now a minus sign after accounting parentheses raises `ValueError`, and `Decimal()` conversion failures are wrapped into `ValueError` too (`InvalidOperation` is an `ArithmeticError`, and notably a `ValueError` *subclass*, so wrapping is what makes the contract explicit). `"(45.00)" → Decimal("-45.00")` is preserved, as are `"-45.00"`, `"(1,234.50)"`, `"€ 3.10"`.

**2. `Ledger.between` — end exclusion** (`ledger/core.py:65`)
`start <= e.day < end` → `start <= e.day <= end`, matching the documented inclusive docstring.

**3. `monthly_totals` — key padding** (`ledger/core.py:68`)
`f"{e.day.year}-{e.day.month}"` → `f"{e.day.year:04d}-{e.day.month:02d}"`. This also silently broke ordering, not just formatting: `"2026-3" > "2026-10"` as strings, so October sorted before March. Zero-padding makes the existing `dict(sorted(...))` produce correct ascending order.

**4. `apply_discount`** — already correct; `Decimal("19.99") × 0.85 = 16.9915` rounds half-up to `16.99`. Left untouched and added `test_discount_half_up_to_cents` as a guard against future rounding-mode regressions.

No public names or signatures changed (verified: no `def` lines in the diff).
